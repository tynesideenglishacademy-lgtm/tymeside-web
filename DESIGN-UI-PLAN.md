# Tyneside English Academy: design and UI plan

## Design diagnosis

The current site is competent but visually generic. Its main signals are a floating glass navigation bar, a dark photographic hero, repeated rounded cards, icon tiles, centred section headings, and amber accents on navy. Those patterns are common in generated landing pages, so the academy's own character is being hidden by the interface.

The redesign preserves the information architecture, URLs, forms, integrations, legal copy, and bilingual content. It replaces the presentation system.

## Design position

**Working direction:** North East editorial school.

Tyneside should feel like a real independent educational institution with a strong connection to Newcastle and a practical presence in Murcia. The reference is a contemporary cultural institution or well-designed school prospectus, not a software landing page.

**Design dials**

- Visual variance: 7/10. Asymmetric compositions and varied content rhythms.
- Motion: 3/10. Short reveal and hover feedback only.
- Density: 5/10. More useful information per viewport, with generous reading space.
- Theme: light editorial throughout, with navy used for the masthead and footer.

## Visual system

### Colour

- Paper: `#F3F1EA`
- White: `#FCFCF8`
- Ink navy: `#102A3A`
- River teal: `#087F83`
- Gold detail: `#C7A646`
- Muted slate: `#5E6B70`

Teal is the functional colour for links, focus, and primary actions. Gold is limited to small brand details and accreditation context.

### Typography

- Headings use a condensed, editorial sans-serif stack.
- Body copy uses a familiar humanist system stack for clarity.
- Headlines are sentence case, left aligned, and shorter.
- Labels are used only when they add navigation or context.

### Shape and depth

- Corners are square or lightly rounded at 2-4px.
- Cards are replaced with layout, rules, and spacing wherever possible.
- Shadows are rare. Image depth comes from cropping and composition.
- No glass panels, decorative gradients, floating pills, or icon tiles.

## Page structure

1. Solid masthead with direct navigation and one primary action.
2. Asymmetric split hero with Tyne Bridge photography, concise promise, level test, and verifiable proof.
3. Course programme index using varied photographic blocks.
4. Academy statement and methodology evidence.
5. Cambridge exam preparation and institutional credentials.
6. One prominent review and compact proof summary.
7. Services directory with clear routes to forms and dossier.
8. Careers callout and private CV form.
9. Newcastle and North East educational travel presented as a photo essay.
10. Contact form, map, and direct details.

## Interaction rules

- No scroll effects for decoration.
- Images zoom by no more than 2 percent on hover.
- Links use underline movement or colour change.
- Buttons move by no more than 1px.
- All animation respects reduced-motion preferences.
- Mobile uses a full-width, high-contrast menu and keeps the key CTA visible.

## Content rules

- Use real academy information and traceable claims.
- Use Newcastle imagery as place and identity, not as a claim that a class happened there.
- Keep course copy concise and route detailed questions to the relevant form.
- Partner logos appear as credentials in a formal row, with optical rather than mathematical sizing.
- Avoid repeated slogans, duplicated actions, and unsupported statistics.

## Success criteria

- A visitor can identify the academy, location, course range, and next action in the first viewport.
- The page does not resemble a generic SaaS or generated landing page.
- Every section has a distinct content layout while using the same visual system.
- Mobile navigation, forms, legal pages, level test, and service routes continue to work.
- Build, responsive layout, keyboard navigation, focus visibility, and reduced motion are verified before release.
